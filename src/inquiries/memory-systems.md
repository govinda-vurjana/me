Remembering, retrieving, and thinking are usually treated as separate problems: storage, search, and reasoning. I want to understand how they connect. What does it mean for a system to remember something, how does it find what it needs again, and how does that become thinking?

I expect to come at it from several directions as they open up: recurrent networks and what their state actually holds, associative memory, dynamical systems, and the mathematics underneath them. Each direction gets its own inquiry and timeline when I start on it.
