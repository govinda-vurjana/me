This is the line of research underneath most of what I do.

Remembering, retrieving, and thinking are usually treated as separate problems: storage, search, and reasoning. I want to understand how they connect: what it means for a system to remember something, how it finds what it needs again, and how that becomes thinking.

I expect to come at it from several directions as they open up: recurrent networks and what their state actually holds, associative memory, dynamical systems, and the mathematics underneath them. Each direction gets its own inquiry and timeline when I start on it.
