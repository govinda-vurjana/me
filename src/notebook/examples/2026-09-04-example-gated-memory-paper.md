abstract: >
  This is an example abstract. Recurrent networks are often described as storing their past in a hidden
  state. We argue instead that what a recurrent network remembers is determined by what it is permitted to
  forget, and show on a family of delayed-recall tasks that gating converts a fading summary into a channel
  that persists across hundreds of steps.
